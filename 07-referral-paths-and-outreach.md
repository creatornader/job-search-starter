# Prompt 7: Research Referral Paths and Draft Outreach

Run this for a role the candidate has chosen to pursue. Referral research can happen in parallel with application preparation when a deadline is close.

```text
Research whether there is a credible referral or conversation path for this role. Use only the candidate's supplied information, sources they approved, and public information. If a LinkedIn, email, or other account is not already connected with explicit permission, ask the candidate what source they want to use. Do not attempt to bypass access controls or scrape private data.

For each possible contact, record:
- current employer and role, with a source and date checked
- the path: direct relationship, mutual connection, shared school or community, shared professional work, recruiter, or cold contact
- what the candidate says about the actual relationship, separate from what a profile implies
- the evidence for the path and confidence level
- relevance to the role or team, without assuming the person knows the hiring team
- any public evidence about referral mechanics; mark unknown process details as unknown

Rank the paths by warmth and relevance. Do not describe a new connection, a mutual, or an alumnus as a friend or close contact unless the candidate confirms that relationship. Do not assume that a person can or will refer the candidate.

For the best paths, draft an individualized message suited to the relationship and platform. Give context before the exact job title when the recipient may not know the role; use a concise plain-language description followed by the title in parentheses. Keep within any connection-note or form character limit and report the count. Include a clear, low-pressure ask and a reason the person is a sensible contact. Draft a follow-up only when timing supports one.

Return the contact map, source notes, message drafts, recommended order, and a referral-versus-direct timing plan. Do not send a connection request, message, email, referral request, or follow-up. The candidate reviews each draft and approves any send separately.
```

## Decision rule

Do not wait indefinitely for a referral. Set a direct-application cutoff based on the official posting, stated deadline, recruiter guidance, and response timing. If the cutoff arrives, recommend the next step and let the candidate decide.
