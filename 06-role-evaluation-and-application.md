# Prompt 6: Evaluate One Role and Prepare a Review Package

```text
Evaluate this role using the candidate evidence layer and the official job description: [PASTE OFFICIAL URL OR JD]. Verify that it is live first.

Use Career-Ops' evaluation and tracker conventions as reference: https://github.com/santifer/career-ops. Use AI Job Search's drafter-reviewer, PDF-text, interview, and outcome workflow as a second check when useful: https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search.

Return:

1. A one-sentence verdict and fit score with decisive strengths and gaps.
2. A role thesis: the one or two parts of the candidate's background that should lead.
3. A factual mapping from job requirements to evidence, with unsupported requirements marked as gaps.
4. A recommendation on referral or direct application timing.
5. A tailored resume plan that preserves the candidate's approved visual template.
6. Draft application answers and outreach copy only if the candidate asks for them.
7. A submission-readiness checklist covering resume, questions, work authorization, compensation, links, and requested attachments.

If the candidate approves tailoring, create a dated application archive containing the original job description, targeted editable resume, submission PDF, answers, research, and a decision log. Render and inspect the final PDF. Stop before external submission or sending outreach.
```
