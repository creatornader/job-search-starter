# Prompt 6: Evaluate One Role

Run this only after the candidate approves the evidence base and discovery settings. Evaluate one role at a time.

```text
Evaluate this role using the approved candidate evidence base and the employer's official posting: [PASTE OFFICIAL URL OR JOB DESCRIPTION]. Verify that the posting is live and that the application path works. Record when and where you checked.

Use Career-Ops as the primary reference for role evaluation and tracking: https://github.com/santifer/career-ops. Use AI Job Search as a second review for ranking and outcome learning: https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search.

Do not tailor a resume or draft outreach yet. Return:

1. A go / monitor / skip recommendation with the reasons that could change it.
2. The role's purpose, decision scope, expected outcomes, level, reporting line, location, work arrangement, compensation, required materials, and any time pressure. Mark details not found in the posting.
3. A requirement-to-evidence map. Cite evidence IDs and source locations. Separate direct evidence, adjacent evidence, and gaps.
4. A whole-candidate fit view. Consider career direction, role title and level, dates and overlaps, likely interpretation of the candidate's progression, and which experience or projects would need explanation. Do not optimize for keyword overlap alone.
5. The strongest role-specific candidate thesis and the main risk in the application.
6. Whether a referral path should be explored before applying, what must be verified, and a direct-application cutoff tied to the posting's state and timing.
7. The estimated work needed to prepare an accurate application and the unresolved questions for the candidate.

Save the posting, source notes, liveness check, fit memo, and next action in the candidate's private application tracker. Stop and ask the candidate whether to skip, monitor, or proceed to referral research and application preparation.
```

## Review with the candidate

Do not treat a high score as permission to apply. The candidate chooses whether the role is worth pursuing and whether to invest in a tailored packet.
