# Prompt 9: Preflight, Submit, and Follow Through

Use this after the candidate has reviewed the tailored application package. This prompt covers portal readiness and post-application tracking. It does not itself authorize a submission.

```text
Reopen the employer's official application path and compare the live form with the saved field inventory. Check each field, exact answer, limit, required/optional state, attachment, and any change since the earlier review. Confirm the resume PDF is the approved version and the editable file matches it.

Return a preflight checklist covering:
- identity, contact, location, work authorization, sponsorship, and availability
- each application question and its approved answer
- every requested or optional document, link, or work sample
- resume filename, page count, visual render, readable text, and working links
- salary, office attendance, relocation, prior-employment, legal, arbitration, and voluntary demographic questions
- referral status and the direct-application cutoff

Never infer or automatically answer personal, legal, demographic, or consent questions. Ask the candidate to decide each unresolved item. Do not paste or upload a draft into an external form without the candidate's approval for that application.

If anything is missing, contradictory, stale, or different from the approved packet, stop and report it. Otherwise show the exact final package and ask the candidate for explicit permission to submit this specific application. Without that approval, stop at ready-to-submit. If the candidate approves and the tools allow submission, submit only this application, then verify the confirmation screen or email and record the receipt, timestamp, exact materials, and status. If verification fails, say so and do not claim it was submitted.

After an approved submission, update the private tracker with next steps and dates. Record referral or recruiter replies, follow-up timing, interview stages, preparation needs, rejection, offer, or withdrawal. For interviews, build preparation from the actual role, employer guidance, submitted materials, and evidence IDs. Keep outcome feedback distinct from confirmed career facts; ask the candidate before changing the evidence base.
```

## Stop conditions

- No explicit approval for this application: do not submit.
- Legal, demographic, relocation, compensation, or office-work answer unresolved: ask the candidate.
- No verified confirmation: record the submission state as unknown, not complete.
- A referral arrives after direct application: check whether it can attach to the existing application before creating anything new.
